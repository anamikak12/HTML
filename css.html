KMCT

About

KMCT was established in 1994 by Dr. K. Moidu, a leading medical practitioner and visionary educationist. What began as a single pharmacy college has grown into nine institutions across two campuses — engineering, nursing, pharmacy, architecture, business, polytechnic, teacher education, and teacher training.

Every programme is approved by the national body that regulates it: AICTE for engineering and management, KTU for B.Tech, INC and KNC for nursing, COA for architecture, PCI for pharmacy, and NCTE for teaching programmes.
