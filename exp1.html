Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of a computer system or machine to mimic human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making. Instead of following fixed rules, AI systems study data to find patterns and make decisions. They power everyday tools like voice assistants, map navigation, and translation apps.

  1. Data Processing:

    2. AI systems use algorithms—step-by-step instructions—to process large amounts of data.

  2. Pattern Recognition:

    3. Computers look for regularities and patterns in information instead of thinking with human consciousness.

  3. Machine Learning:

    4. Systems improve their performance over time automatically by learning from new experiences.
